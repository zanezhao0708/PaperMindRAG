### 1. ADClaw：面向工业异常检测与理解的可编程视觉智能体 ★★★★☆

**原题**: ADClaw: A Programmable Visual Agent for Industrial Anomaly Detection and Understanding  
**作者**: Xinyi Wu, Rongxin Gao, YuHui Lin, Yonglin Hong, Chu Li, Wei Li 等 10 人  
**提交**: 2026-11-25 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34591)
**主题**: 工业、多模态  

- **做了什么**: 提出无需任务微调的可编程视觉智能体ADClaw，通过编写并执行代码反复检验异常假设，实现工业异常检测、定位与理解。
- **亮点**: 结构化正常先验检索规则与正常参考；持久化检查工作台组合掩码、异常图与测量；假设引导证据推理驱动下一步代码动作。
- **原文摘要**: Industrial anomaly detection (IAD), the task of identifying and localizing deviations from normal product conditions, is essential to automated quality inspection in manufacturing. Many vision-language models for IAD rely on large domain-specific datasets for fine-tuning and make predictions in a single pass, leaving no opportunity to re-examine ambiguous regions. Although tool-using agents can revisit these regions, predefined tool interfaces limit how they can combine intermediate results in a follow-up check.
