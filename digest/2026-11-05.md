### 1. 复现INP-Former：重建内在正常原型用于工业异常检测 ★★★☆☆

**原题**: Reproducing INP-Former: Reconstructing Intrinsic Normal Prototypes for Industrial Anomaly Detection  
**作者**: Yunfei Liang, Juan Pablo Osorio Rivera  
**提交**: 2026-11-01 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01230)
**主题**: 工业、基准  

- **做了什么**: 复现INP-Former并扩展评估到MVTec AD 2，发现多数性能来自INP机制，但默认原型数等结论在更难数据上不成立。
- **亮点**: 复现核心结论并做五种子消融、双损失对比、原型数迁移性分析，揭示默认六原型在MVTec AD 2上不如单原型。
- **原文摘要**: We reproduce the central claims of INP-Former, a reconstruction-based method for multi-class industrial anomaly detection. INP-Former extracts Intrinsic Normal Prototypes (INPs) from each test image and uses them to guide a decoder that reconstructs normal features, without a stored memory bank. Using the authors' released code, we reproduce the reported performance on MVTec AD and VisA and extend the evaluation to MVTec AD 2, where image AUROC with the default settings is 63.4, compared with 99.7 on MVTec AD.
