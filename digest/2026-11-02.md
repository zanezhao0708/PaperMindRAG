### 1. VariaFlow：面向无监督异常检测的不确定性感知流匹配 ★★★★☆

**原题**: VariaFlow: Uncertainty-Aware Flow Matching for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection  
**作者**: Arnela Hadzic, Martin Urschler, Cosmin I. Bercea  
**提交**: 2026-10-30 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41494)
**主题**: 医学、基准  

- **做了什么**: 提出VariaFlow，用流匹配模型集成估计健康重建的预测方差并归一化残差，实现跨数据集脑MRI无监督异常检测。
- **亮点**: 用掩码引导恢复集成估计逐位置预测方差，归一化残差区分病变与解剖变异；少步流匹配降低开销，双尺度恢复捕捉不同大小病灶。
- **原文摘要**: Reconstruction-based anomaly detection no longer fails at reconstruction. Generative models produce high-quality pseudo-healthy reconstructions, and the remaining difficulty is the step of scoring residuals. The residual between a scan and its reconstruction has two indistinguishable sources, genuine pathology and the anatomical variation that separates one healthy brain from another. Scoring against a single reconstruction treats both as anomalous, inflating false positives where healthy anatomy varies most while small or subtle lesions are lost in the surrounding reconstruction error.
