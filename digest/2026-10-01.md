### 1. PCB-MC：印刷电路板缺失元件分析 ★★★★☆

**原题**: PCB-MC: Missing Component Analysis in Printed Circuit Boards  
**作者**: Betsy Villa Brochero, Ian Gibson, Estefania Talavera  
**提交**: 2026-09-30 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39427)
**主题**: 工业、基准  

- **做了什么**: 提出PCB-MC数据集，用于检测印刷电路板上缺失的元件，并提供基准测试揭示现有监督与无监督方法在此任务上的严重不足。
- **亮点**: 构建197种板型、足迹级标注的缺失元件检测数据集，提出防布局泄漏的板型感知交叉验证，并给出多方法基准。
- **原文摘要**: Detecting missing components on printed circuit boards (PCBs) differs fundamentally from conventional object detection, as the model must localize components that are not present. We introduce PCB-MC, a curated dataset for missing component detection with footprint level annotations built on top of the RF100 dataset. The dataset contains 197 distinct board types, each corresponding to a unique PCB design, with multiple augmented samples per type.
