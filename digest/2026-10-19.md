### 1. 知识引导的层次化分布提示学习用于零样本异常检测 ★★★★☆

**原题**: Knowledge-Guided Distributional Prompt Learning with Visual Hierarchy for Zero-Shot Anomaly Detection  
**作者**: Jiyul Ham, Seongho Yoon, Yonggon Jung, Sangjae Lee, Jun-Geol Baek  
**提交**: 2026-10-16 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23172)
**主题**: 工业、医学  

- **做了什么**: 提出HiDist框架，用正态参考的层次化分布提示建模，将异常提示参数化为对正常参考的偏离，实现零样本异常检测与定位。
- **亮点**: 提示token建模为对角高斯分布并蒙特卡洛采样；异常分布参数化为正常参考的偏离；全局/局部层次化表示分别用于检测与定位。
- **原文摘要**: Zero-shot anomaly detection (ZSAD) aims to detect anomalies in previously unseen classes using auxiliary images, with applications such as industrial visual inspection and medical imaging. Recent vision-language-model-based approaches improve ZSAD by aligning textual and visual representations, while distributional prompting further models prompt variability for unseen categories.
