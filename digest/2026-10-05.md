### 1. 面向高效海洋环境监测的星载异常检测 ★★★☆☆

**原题**: On-Board Anomaly Detection for Efficient Marine Environmental Monitoring  
**作者**: Thomas Goudemant, Clotilde Szywala, Benjamin Francesconi, Michelle Aubrun, Yves Bobichon, Marjorie Bellizzi 等 7 人  
**提交**: 2026-10-03 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03649)
**主题**: 工业、多模态  

- **做了什么**: 提出一种面向多/高光谱地球观测卫星的海洋事件检测流水线，用自监督编码器压缩图像并在星上完成异常检测，以节省带宽并提升响应速度。
- **亮点**: 自监督编码器压缩图像至低维隐空间，结合轻量异常检测模型，适配嵌入式CPU到AI加速器，已在ESA Phisat-2等任务集成。
- **原文摘要**: Marine ecosystems are impacted by various threats such as oil spills, algal blooms, and sediment floods, which disrupt habitats, wildlife, and human activities. Advances in satellite imagery and Artificial Intelligence (AI) have enhanced our capabilities for early detection and mitigation of such hazards. In this paper, we propose a marine event detection pipeline for Earth observation satellites equipped with multi- or hyperspectral sensors.
