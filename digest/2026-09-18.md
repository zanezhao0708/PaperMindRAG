### 1. 从正常中看见异常：正常肾形态表征中的肾小球异常 ★★★★☆

**原题**: Seeing Abnormal from Normal: Glomerular Abnormality in Representations of Normal Renal Morphology  
**作者**: Greta Hasko, Rachit Saluja, Tianyu Shi, Leiyue Zhao, Yuechen Yang, Daniel Reisenbuechler 等 16 人

Supervised classification requires labeled examples of every category, which is impractical when subtypes are rare or absent from the training cohort. One-class anomaly detection offers an alternative by modeling normal data and scoring deviations, allowing previously unseen abnormalities to be detected.
