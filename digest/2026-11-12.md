### 1. REDiff-AD：无需训练与目标掩码的多样性驱动上下文MMDiT工业异常图像生成 ★★★★☆

**原题**: REDiff-AD: No Training and No Target Masks for Industrial Anomaly Image Generation via Diversity-Driven In-Context MMDiT  
**作者**: Chih-Yu Tsai, Chih-Heng Chang, Jian-Jiun Ding  
**提交**: 2026-11-08 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10875)
**主题**: 工业、基准  

- **做了什么**: 提出REDiff-AD框架，仅需一张正常产品图和单个带掩码参考异常，无需训练和目标掩码即可生成真实多样的工业异常图像并自动定位伪掩码。
- **亮点**: 引入Rectified Flow MMDiT实现少步高效采样；复用内部注意力信号定位合成异常，结合边缘与颜色线索细化像素级伪掩码；自适应参考条件与多样性引导模块免训练提升保真度与多样性。
- **原文摘要**: Industrial anomaly detection is challenged by the scarcity and diversity of real defects, leaving insufficient anomalous data to effectively train downstream models. Existing anomaly generation methods often require product-specific fine-tuning, target mask annotation, or costly procedures to achieve realistic and diverse defects. We propose REDiff-AD, a framework that requires neither model training nor target masks and introduces Rectified Flow MMDiT into industrial anomaly generation for efficient sampling with fewer generation steps.
