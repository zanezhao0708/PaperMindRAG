### 1. VerdictAD：决策引导细化的少样本异常检测 ★★★★☆

**原题**: VerdictAD: Few-Shot Anomaly Detection via Decision-Guided Refinement  
**作者**: Shangquan Sun, Wenjun Luo, Yuanzhi Wang, Ruiqi Li, Ruoyu Chen, Jiachen Liu 等 7 人  
**提交**: 2026-11-02 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02654)
**主题**: 工业、少样本  

- **做了什么**: 提出免训练框架VerdictAD，用类型化决策模型对查询图像及局部区域给出结构化决策概率，从全局和局部两个尺度细化现有异常检测器的异常分数。
- **亮点**: 免训练、即插即用；类型化决策模型直接输出结构化概率而非文本生成；全局与局部判决细化模块互补增强现有检测器。
- **原文摘要**: Detecting defects in real-world industrial inspection often requires learning from only a few normal images per category. Recent few-shot anomaly detection methods have achieved strong performance by measuring feature differences between query images and normal references. However, visual differences do not always indicate genuine defects, as normal appearance variations can also produce high anomaly scores.
