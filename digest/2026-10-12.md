### 1. 荧光显微镜图像中极稀有死细胞的检测 ★★★☆☆

**原题**: Detection of extremely rare dead cells in fluorescence microscopy images  
**作者**: Prateek Verma, Benjamin Butter, Jian Zhang, Xintao Wu  
**提交**: 2026-10-10 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13780)
**主题**: 医学、少样本  

- **做了什么**: 系统比较启发式、分类与异常检测方法在荧光显微图像中检测极稀有凋亡细胞，发现ResNet-18和XGBoost表现最佳但仍具挑战。
- **亮点**: 在258张图、65527个细胞、仅243个死细胞的极不平衡数据上系统对比三类方法，深度学习与XGBoost优于启发式和异常检测。
- **原文摘要**: Dead cell detection in fluorescence microscopy is essential for phenotypic screening and drug efficacy assessment. However, automated identification remains challenging due to signal variability, optical artifacts, and colocalization ambiguities across multiple channels. Markers that define nuclear boundaries do not neatly coincide spatially or temporally with apoptotic markers. Furthermore, marker accumulation kinetics vary across individual cells and apoptosis stages, making universal thresholds unreliable.
