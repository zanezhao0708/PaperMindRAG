**原题**: Region-VAD: Video Anomaly Localization and Detection via Region Selection  
**作者**: Giacomo D'Amicantonio  
**提交**: 2026-11-16 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21132)
**主题**: 视频、基准  

- **做了什么**: 提出Region-VAD，先在冻结基础模型token网格上选出变化最大的矩形区域，再仅用区域内token做异常打分，实现弱监督视频异常定位与检测。
- **亮点**: 无需框/掩码标注，用冻结特征相邻段变化穷举搜索选区域，仅训练池化与时间头，定位与检测共享一套配置。
- **原文摘要**: Anomalies are local events, yet weakly supervised video anomaly detection scores them globally. Most methods pool an entire frame into a single vector, rank frames by that vector, and derive a location afterwards as a saliency map of the score. Full-frame pooling mixes the anomaly with its background, so a detector can reach competitive benchmark scores from scene context without being able to show where in the scene the anomaly occurs.
