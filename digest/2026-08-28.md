### 1. GeoMAD：基于可变形融合与分布对齐的几何感知多视角异常检测 ★★★★☆

**原题**: GeoMAD: Geometry-Aware Multi-View Anomaly Detection via Deformable Fusion and Distributional Alignment  
**作者**: Shang-Fu Chen, Jhih-Ciang Wu, Kuan-Chuan Peng, Wen-Huang Cheng, Kai-Lung Hua  
**提交**: 2026-08-27 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26724)
**主题**: 工业、3D  

- **做了什么**: 提出统一的多视角多类别异常检测框架GeoMAD，通过跨视角可变形融合模块学习连续几何对应，并引入分布对齐正则化，无需相机标定或3D监督即可实现高效且几何感知的融合。
- **亮点**: 跨视角可变形融合模块(CDFM)学习内容自适应采样偏移，构建多尺度窗口金字塔；分布视角对齐(DVA)自监督正则化对齐各视角瓶颈分布，兼顾局部几何与全局一致性。
- **原文摘要**: Multi-view anomaly detection (MvAD) detects defects by exploiting complementary observations from multiple camera viewpoints. The central challenge is to fuse views with sufficient geometric awareness while remaining scalable to multi-class industrial settings. Existing methods typically fall into two extremes: voxel-based fusion provides explicit geometric alignment but requires costly 3D construction and class-specific assumptions, whereas lightweight patch-based fusion is efficient but relies on discrete candidate matching and lacks continuous cross-view correspondence.
