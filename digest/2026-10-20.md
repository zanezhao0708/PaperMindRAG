### 1. YUTELA：面向多站点视频异常检测的可扩展边缘云AIoT系统 ★★★★☆

**作者**: Shanle Yao, Babak Rahimi Ardabili, Amir Kimiyaie, Hamed Tabkhi

- **做了什么**: 提出YUTELA边缘-服务器-云AIoT系统，用Jetson Thor边缘设备替代工作站，实现多站点长期视频异常检测与场景自适应重训练。

- **原文摘要**: Building upon our previous Ancilia framework, this work presents \emph{YUTELA}, an edge--server--cloud AIoT system for long-term, multi-site video anomaly detection. YUTELA replaces workstation-based on-site processing with NVIDIA Jetson Thor edge devices and redesigns the multi-camera pipeline through camera batching and redistribution, shared memory, reduced inter-stage data transfer, task integration, and TensorRT-accelerated inference.
