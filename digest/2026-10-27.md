### 1. SGDB：面向逻辑异常检测的语义-几何解耦与证据绑定 ★★★★☆

**原题**: SGDB: Semantic-Geometric Decoupling and Evidence Binding for Logical Anomaly Detection  
**作者**: Chenrui Ji, Jiafan Lu, Zilu Luo, Feng Wan, Qiuhui Chen, Weimin Zhong  
**提交**: 2026-10-26 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34897)
**主题**: 工业、多模态  

- **做了什么**: 提出语义-几何解耦与证据绑定框架SGDB，通过分离物体身份识别与几何测量并绑定证据，实现可解释的逻辑异常检测。
- **亮点**: 语义解码器学身份、独立读取器做测量；部分一对一关联绑定几何证据；支持认证读取器选轴；执行 grounded 解释生成紧凑告警证书。
- **原文摘要**: Logical anomaly detection requires recognizing scene components and measuring whether their configuration satisfies task constraints. Correct identities can coexist with inaccurate geometry; clean mask fragments can yield misleading lengths. We propose Semantic-Geometric Decoupling and Evidence Binding (SGDB). A semantic decoder learns object identities from component-annotated normal sources and factual edits; a separate reader supplies final measurements.
