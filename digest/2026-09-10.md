### 1. 卷积神经网络的分层可调提升方案 ★★★☆☆

**原题**: Layerwise Tunable Lifting Scheme for the Convolutional Neural Network  
**作者**: Abdumannon Yovkochov, An Le, Sungbal Seo, You-Suk Bae, Truong Nguyen  
**提交**: 2026-09-09 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09827)
**主题**: 工业、基准  

- **做了什么**: 提出三种基于格结构的可调提升小波滤波器组策略，嵌入ResNet-18后在纹理分类和工业异常检测任务上取得稳定提升。
- **亮点**: 格结构保证任意参数下可逆稳定；支持低通、高通及顺序联合调优三种策略。
- **原文摘要**: This work introduces a family of tunable lifting schemes for biorthogonal wavelet filter banks. We propose three lifting strategies: low-pass tuning (LS-LayLatt-LP), high-pass tuning (LS-LayLatt-HP), and a sequential lifting scheme that jointly adapts low- and high-frequency branches (LS-LayLatt-Sequential). All proposed designs are formulated using a lattice-based lifting structure, which guarantees invertibility and stability for arbitrary parameter values within the lifting functions.
