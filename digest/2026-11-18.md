To unlock uncurated clinical data and scale analysis, methods are needed to detect abnormalities across contrasts and resolutions. Synthetic data has provided robust, contrast-agnostic models for a variety of human neuroimaging tasks in healthy anatomy, but pathology detection and other tasks in clinical settings still require real patient data, contrast-specific models, or disease-specific generators.
