### 1. 有界AUKS：一种基于有界Kolmogorov-Smirnov曲线下面积的异常检测新评价指标 ★★★★☆

**原题**: Bounded AUKS: A Novel Evaluation Metric for Anomaly Detection via Bounded Area Under the Kolmogorov--Smirnov Curve  
**作者**: Yanshuo Zhang, Juan Du  
**提交**: 2026-11-03 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04754)
**主题**: 工业、基准  

- **做了什么**: 提出Bounded AUKS指标，通过数据驱动的有界区间积分TPR与FPR之差，量化正常与异常样本异常分数分布的可分性，弥补AUROC/AUPRO忽略阈值选择的缺陷。
- **亮点**: 从KS积分引入AUKS直接度量分布可分性；下界排除高FPR无信息区，上界避免极端低FPR导致低召回；区间仅由正常测试样本分数分布确定。
- **原文摘要**: Recent literature widely used global metrics such as AUROC and AUPRO to quantify the detection performance. AUROC and AUPRO scores are approaching 100% in some public datasets such as MVTec AD dataset and VisA dataset, which misleadingly suggests that the pixel-level anomaly detection task has already been solved. Threshold-independent metrics represented by AUROC inherently ignore the threshold selection process, which is inevitable in practical applications.
