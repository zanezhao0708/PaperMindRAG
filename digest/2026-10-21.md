### 1. AnomalyJev：通过声明级验证评估图像异常解释 ★★★★☆

- **原文摘要**: Recent multimodal LLM (MLLM)-based anomaly detection (AD) methods can generate textual explanations that help interpret their predictions. However, reliably and efficiently evaluating these explanations remains challenging. While LLM judges can assess explanations through structured rubrics, they often require substantial token generation, making evaluation costly and difficult to scale. We instead propose to anchor evaluation in the available ground truth by extracting verifiable claims from each explanation and verifying them against the corresponding annotations.
