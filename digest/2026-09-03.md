### 1. DPA：解耦产品无关的异常表征用于零样本异常生成 ★★★★☆

**原题**: DPA: Decoupling Product-Agnostic Anomaly Representations for Zero-shot Anomaly Generation  
**作者**: Hang Yao, Yansheng Fu, Ming Liu, Zifei Yan, Yanli Ji, Hongzhi Zhang 等 7 人  
**提交**: 2026-09-02 · [论文链接](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02075)
**主题**: 工业、少样本  

- **做了什么**: 提出一种基于异常迁移的零样本异常生成方法，通过扩散模型解耦产品无关的异常表征，并利用自适应掩码生成逼真异常，提升下游检测性能。
- **亮点**: 提出异常迁移新范式，利用源产品真实异常生成目标产品异常；设计解耦产品无关异常嵌入的扩散框架；引入自适应掩码控制异常位置与几何合理性；无需训练的标注模块生成像素级标签。
- **原文摘要**: Industrial anomaly detection benefits from anomaly samples, yet newly deployed products typically provide only normal images, making anomaly samples difficult to collect. Zero-shot anomaly generation offers a promising solution which avoids collection of target-product anomalies. However, existing methods mainly rely on texture images or text descriptions as anomaly sources, which often produce unrealistic anomalies.
